Imagine you're working in an office with:

100 developers
20 QA engineers
10 DevOps engineers

Without a network:

You can't share files.
You can't access servers.
You can't connect to GitHub.
You can't access databases.
You can't browse the internet.

A network enables all of these communications.

Types of Networks
--------------------
| Network Type | Description               | Example                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| LAN          | Local Area Network        | Office, home            |
| WAN          | Wide Area Network         | Internet, corporate WAN |
| MAN          | Metropolitan Area Network | City-wide networks      |
| PAN          | Personal Area Network     | Bluetooth devices       |


For AWS and DevOps, the most important concepts are LAN and WAN.

Real-World AWS Analogy

Think of your AWS account as a city.

Inside that city, you create a VPC.

The VPC is like a private gated community where only your resources (EC2, RDS, EKS nodes, etc.) live and communicate.

We'll expand this analogy throughout the bootcamp.
